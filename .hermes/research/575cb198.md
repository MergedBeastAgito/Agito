from principle—if a view joins an old grant to the current secret value, that
grant may silently authorize future changes too. A recorded observation tied to

current truth should be a separate, explicit entitlement. Check: change the
secret after a one-time reveal and verify the old observer receives no new

publication especially consequential because hiding a later projection does not

security mechanism, and POMDPs supply inference/planning semantics, not
authorization. Check: demonstrate one end-to-end probe that changes the
